Solar firefly board: schematic, rev 0.1

An all through-hole ATtiny13A board. A 5 V panel charges a 1 F supercap, and one red LED both flashes and senses its neighbours. About 20 parts, no battery and no charging IC.

Energy budget

≈12 Jusable, 5.3 → 1.8 V on 1 F
≈1.5 minto fill in full sun
≈12 hflashing every 3 s
≈4 daysasleep, no flashing

Assumptions: about 8 µA asleep (watchdog, cap divider, supercap leakage), and 20 ms flashes at about 10 mA. Coin supercaps run −20% on capacitance, so treat these as upper bounds and check them on the bench.

Why the parts are where they are

  • D2 sits on the panel side, so its knee leakage only costs panel current and never drains the cap at night.
  • BAT46 for D1: it leaks far less in reverse than a 1N5817, which matters over a 12-hour night.
  • The panel divider draws nothing in the dark, because D1 isolates it from the cap. The cap divider costs about 1 µA all the time.
  • C2 and C1 supply the LED pulse current, because the supercap's 15–30 Ω ESR would sag the rail by about 0.3 V.
  • C3 and C4 hold the high-impedance dividers steady while the ADC samples.

Before layout

  • Check the chip marking. A plain ATtiny13 (not A or V) needs at least 2.7 V.
  • Fuses: 128 kHz or 1.2 MHz clock, BOD at 1.8 V, disabled during sleep in firmware (the 13A supports this).
  • Socket U1 and program the chips off-board. The LED between MOSI and MISO can upset in-circuit programming.
  • The internal 1.1 V reference is only accurate to ±10%, so calibrate thresholds per board or leave margin.
  • Keep the LED at the board edge facing out, and put the panel on the lid with a drain hole below.
